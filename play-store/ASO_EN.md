## Full description

```
Nodak is a number-logic puzzle game built around a simple distance rule.

How to play
• Place only digits 1, 2, 3, and 4
• Each digit has a twin in the same row or column
• Leave exactly that many empty cells between the pair
  (example: a 3 needs three empty cells between twins)

Features
• Easy, medium, and hard levels
• Free play on 6×6 and 8×8 boards
• Helps and hint support
• Themes, languages, and sound settings
• First-launch “How to play” guide

Who is it for?
Puzzle fans and casual players who like number puzzles, logic games, and a quick mental workout.

Download free and start thinking in distance.

FAQ
Q: What happens on a wrong move?
A: It counts as a mistake; when you run out, the round ends (you may continue with an ad).

Q: How do hints work?
A: You get limited helps; after that you can earn +1 by watching an ad.

Q: Is progress saved?
A: Level progress and best times stay on your device.
```

# Production access questionnaire answers

Use these (or lightly personalize) in Play Console when asked after closed testing.

**App:** Nodak  
**Package:** `com.nodak.puzzle`

---

### 1) How did you recruit users for your closed test?
We utilized a paid testing provider to gather detailed feedback. We also reached out to puzzle enthusiasts in our target audience for real-world insights.

### 2) How easy was it to recruit testers?
Easy

### 3) Describe the engagement you received from testers
Testers actively engaged with gameplay, UX, and feature feedback. Their notes guided onboarding improvements, store listing clarity, and a clearer path to rate the app.

### 4) Summary of feedback / how you collected it
Feedback called for stronger ASO text, clearer screenshots, a first-run how-to-play guide, and a Rate button. Collected via tester surveys and direct communication.

### 5) Intended audience
Puzzle enthusiasts and casual players who enjoy number-based logic challenges — all ages looking for a strategic, short-session mental workout.

### 6) How the app provides value
Nodak offers a unique distance-based number puzzle with board sizes and leveled content, free to play, combining challenge and entertainment.

### 7) Expected installs in first year
10k – 100k

### 8) Changes made from closed-test learning
We improved Turkish and English store descriptions for ASO, kept clear phone screenshots and a feature graphic, added a first-launch How to play walkthrough, added Rate the app in Settings, and shipped a build with rewarded ads and a correct advertising ID declaration.

### 9) Why ready for production
After closed testing and feedback-driven updates, gameplay is stable, onboarding is clearer, and the listing better explains the game. Ready for production launch.

### 10) What you did differently this time
We prioritized real tester feedback, ASO, onboarding, and a simple rating path so the release matches what users expect on Google Play.
